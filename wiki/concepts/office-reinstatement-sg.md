# Office Reinstatement (Singapore)

## Definition

The process of restoring a leased commercial or office space to its original condition as stipulated in the tenancy agreement, typically required at lease end before handover to the landlord.

## How It Works

Tenants are legally obligated (via tenancy agreement) to reverse all modifications made during occupancy. This includes:

- Removing partitions, raised floors, carpets
- Restoring ceiling tiles and lighting to original spec
- Patching and repainting walls
- Removing signage and branding
- Reinstating MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) systems
- Removing furniture, fit-out, and IT infrastructure
- Obtaining landlord sign-off on completion

Timeline varies by space size: **~2 weeks** (small office) to **~10 weeks** (large space).

## Market Context (Singapore)

- Demand is tied to commercial lease churn — driven by economic cycles, WFH trends, office downsizing
- CBD, one-north, Changi South, and business parks are key geography
- Grade A office reinstatement commands premium pricing due to landlord spec strictness
- BCA and BizSafe compliance expected by most landlords
- Market is fragmented — many small contractors, few with strong online presence
