## Key Takeaways

1. Brand is **IA Builders & Engineering Pte Ltd** — the domain is a keyword-rich alias, not the company name. This is an SEO play.
2. Primary positioning: "Singapore's Trusted Reinstatement Contractor" with speed ("Fast Turnaround") and compliance as key differentiators.
3. Six-step work process is the main trust mechanism — shows professionalism and handles landlord coordination explicitly.
4. Two certifications displayed: BizSafe Star + ISO 45001:2018. ISO 9001 still being pursued.
5. Pricing is never mentioned on the homepage. Only "competitive pricing" + "free quote."
6. CTAs are all phone/callback-based. No online booking, no pricing calculator.
7. Copy is generic and templated — no personality, no specific numbers (no "500 projects," no "10,000 sqft"), no named clients.
8. Mobile experience unknown — site uses standard contractor template aesthetic.
