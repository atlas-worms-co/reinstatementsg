# IA Builders — Current Site Audit (Pre-Redesign)

## What's Working

| Element | Verdict |
|---------|---------|
| Domain name | Strong — reinstatement.sg is a pure keyword domain, excellent for SEO |
| Certifications | BizSafe Star + ISO 45001 displayed — credible for B2B |
| 6-step process | Good structure — shows sophistication, handles landlord coordination explicitly |
| Service breadth | Covers all reinstatement types (Grade A, industrial, retail, business park) |
| Free quote CTA | Low-friction entry point |
| Alan Lee's 40yr experience | Strong trust signal — buried, should be front and center |
| Solaris one-north project | Impressive — GreenMark Platinum, ahead of schedule — needs more visibility |

## What's Broken / Missing

### Copy problems
- Generic, template-feel writing — no personality, no voice
- Two conflicting "years in business" claims (6 and 7)
- No specific numbers anywhere (no project count, no sqft completed, no client count)
- No testimonials or named clients
- Tagline is a claim ("Trusted") without evidence

### Trust gaps
- Only 3 portfolio projects for a 7-year company — looks thin
- No Google reviews widget or star rating
- ISO 9001 "currently pursuing" — shouldn't be claimed alongside confirmed certs
- No case study depth (no sqft, no timeline, no challenge/solution narrative)

### UX / conversion gaps
- All CTAs are phone-based — no online form with instant response
- No pricing transparency even as ranges (this is a barrier for price-sensitive SMEs)
- No online booking or quote calculator
- No FAQ integration on homepage
- Blog exists but likely thin/unused

### Design gaps (assumed — need screenshot)
- Standard contractor template aesthetic
- Likely not mobile-optimized
- No video (before/after process video would be high-impact)
- No map or Google Business integration visible

## Redesign Priorities (Ranked)

1. **Rewrite copy** — real numbers, real voice, real social proof
2. **Expand portfolio** — 3 projects is not enough; add 10+ with full case study format
3. **Add testimonials** — Google Reviews embed or curated quotes with name+company
4. **Online quote form** — reduce friction, capture leads 24/7
5. **Surface Alan Lee** — 40 years of experience is a differentiator, bury it no more
6. **Consistent messaging** — fix the 6 vs 7 years discrepancy and all vague claims
7. **Mobile-first redesign** — assume 60%+ traffic is mobile
8. **Speed / performance** — fast load = lower bounce for commercial intent searches
